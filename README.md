## Project Summary

This project analyzes global mental health data from 1990–2020, with a focus on Nepal and India. It visualizes global schizophrenia trends and compares the prevalence of several mental illnesses between these two countries.

##  Key Insights

- Schizophrenia is present globally but varies by country.
- Depression is the most prevalent mental illness in both Nepal and India.
- India consistently shows slightly higher rates of depression and schizophrenia.
- The bar graph comparison clearly highlights depression as the leading mental health issue.

##  Conclusion

Mental illness is a growing concern in public health — this analysis highlights the importance of improving diagnosis, care access, and mental health awareness in South Asia.
